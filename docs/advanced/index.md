# Advanced Topics

Everything here is optional. The hosted dashboard at
[africanlandscapefutures.wits.ac.za](https://africanlandscapefutures.wits.ac.za/)
needs none of it.

These pages are for running Decision Theatre on your own machine — for offline
fieldwork, workshops without reliable internet, or working with your own data.

## Pages

- **[Install the application](install-the-application.md)** — Get Decision Theatre running on your machine.
- **[Install a data pack](install-a-data-pack.md)** — Give the application some data to show.

For deploying Decision Theatre as a service for others, see the
[Administrator Guide](../administrator-guide/index.md).
