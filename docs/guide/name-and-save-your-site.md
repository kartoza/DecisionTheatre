# Step 11 — Name and save your site

!!! abstract "Goal"
    Turn the boundary into a saved site you can return to.

## Background

A boundary on its own is not much use tomorrow. Naming it makes it a site: it appears in
your list, keeps its own view layout, and carries its own indicator values.

## Steps

1. Give the site a **Title** — required — and an optional **Description**.

![Naming a new site](../assets/images/screenshots/create-site-details.png)

2. Confirm the auto-captured **Thumbnail**, or replace it by dragging an image in or
   clicking to browse. Maximum 5 MB.
3. Click **Create Site**. You land directly in the map view for your new site.

### Your sites

![Your Sites page](../assets/images/screenshots/sites-page.jpg)

Each card shows the thumbnail, title, description and creation date. Hovering reveals
**Edit**, **Clone** and **Delete**; clicking the card body opens the site.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Restores your saved layout, scenarios and factor selections |
| **Edit** | Change title, description and thumbnail |
| **Clone** | Pre-fills the wizard with this site's boundary under a new name |
| **Delete** | Permanent, with a confirmation prompt |

!!! note "Demo sites"
    The four built-in demo sites never show edit, clone or delete — they are meant to stay
    in their demonstration state.

!!! success "What you achieved"
    - Your site is saved and appears in Your Sites
    - You can reopen it with your layout restored
    - You know how to edit, clone and delete sites
