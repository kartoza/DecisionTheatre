# Step 6 — Compare two scenarios

!!! abstract "Goal"
    Use the swipe divider to read one scenario against another.

## Background

The map always renders two synchronised instances, one per scenario. The divider between
them is a handle you drag, so you can move the boundary of the comparison to wherever your
question is.

Because both halves share a viewport, panning and zooming keep them aligned.

## Steps

1. With two scenarios chosen, find the vertical divider across the map.
2. Drag it left or right to reveal more of either side.
3. Drag it within about 3% of an edge and it docks fully to that edge, showing one
   scenario alone.
4. Read the floating labels along the top — they name each scenario and the active factor.

Pan and zoom as usual; both sides move together.

!!! tip "Hiding the divider"
    The **Toggle map swiper** control (columns icon) hides the divider entirely when you
    want a single uninterrupted view.

!!! success "What you achieved"
    - You can drag the divider to compare two scenarios
    - You know how to dock it to one side
    - You know both halves stay synchronised as you navigate
