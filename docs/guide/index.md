## The steps

### Get started

1. **[Open the dashboard](open-the-dashboard.md)** — Get to Decision Theatre in your browser. Nothing to install.
2. **[Find your way around](find-your-way-around.md)** — Recognise the landing page and know the ways in.
3. **[Take a guided tour](take-a-guided-tour.md)** — See the application fully populated before building anything yourself.

### Explore the data

4. **[Open the map](open-the-map.md)** — Get to the map workspace and learn its controls.
5. **[Choose what to show](choose-what-to-show.md)** — Select a factor, two scenarios, and the area to summarise over.
6. **[Compare two scenarios](compare-two-scenarios.md)** — Use the swipe divider to read one scenario against another.
7. **[Identify a catchment](identify-a-catchment.md)** — Inspect the numbers behind a single catchment.
8. **[Read the data four ways](read-the-data-four-ways.md)** — Switch a pane between map, chart, dial and table.
9. **[Work with several panes](work-with-several-panes.md)** — Use grid view to watch several factors at once.

### Build your own

10. **[Define a site boundary](define-a-site-boundary.md)** — Mark out the study area you actually care about.
11. **[Name and save your site](name-and-save-your-site.md)** — Turn the boundary into a saved site you can return to.
12. **[Review your indicators](review-your-indicators.md)** — Read the indicator values computed for your site.
13. **[Set target values](set-target-values.md)** — Say what you are aiming for, and see what it implies.
14. **[Refine a boundary](refine-a-boundary.md)** — Adjust a site's shape after the fact.
