# Step 2 — Find your way around

!!! abstract "Goal"
    Recognise the landing page and know the ways in.

## Background

The landing page is the application's front door. It offers several routes: straight into
the data, into a guided tour, or into your own saved study areas.

Nothing here changes any data, so it is a safe place to look around.

## Steps

1. Launch the application. You arrive at **Welcome to the Landscape Decision Dashboard**.

![The landing page](../assets/images/screenshots/landing-page.jpg)

2. Note the ways in:

    - **Explore the Future of Ecosystem Decision-Making** — opens the map on the full
      dataset with no site loaded
    - **The FEFA and Rewild Capital Partnership** — background on the organisations behind
      the tool
    - **Four audience cards** — each starts a guided tour through a prepared example site
    - **Guided Tour** — restarts the application's own onboarding walkthrough

3. The onboarding walkthrough starts automatically the first time you open the
   application. Step through it with **Next**, or dismiss it with **Skip tour**.

### The header

Once you are past the landing page, the header is your constant navigation:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Back to the landing page |
| **My Sites** (pin) | Your saved study areas |
| **Indicators** (bar chart) | The full indicator table for the open site |
| **Tiles** badge | Green when map tile data is loaded |
| **Edit site boundary** (pencil) | Boundary editing, on the map page |
| **Documentation** (help) | Opens this documentation beside the application |

!!! success "What you achieved"
    - You can recognise the landing page and its four routes in
    - You know what each header control does
    - You know how to restart the onboarding walkthrough
